Privacy policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

This policy explains how information is handled when you visit the Near Nature website.

This policy covers this informational website only. It does not describe data handling in the Near Nature app. An app-specific privacy policy must be provided before the app collects personal information.

Information this website collects

This website has no accounts, contact forms, advertising, or analytics tools. Its code does not collect your name, email address, precise location, photos, or other personal information. The wildlife card interaction takes place in your browser and is not sent to us or saved.

Hosting

This website is intended to be hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit a GitHub Pages site, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes. GitHub handles this hosting information under its own policies. Read GitHub’s explanation of Pages hosting and the GitHub Privacy Statement for more information.

Cookies and browser storage

This website’s code does not set cookies or use local storage. Fonts, illustrations, styles, and scripts are served with the website; no third-party embeds are loaded.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell or share personal information collected through this website, and we do not maintain a visitor database. GitHub’s handling and retention of hosting records are governed by its privacy statement.

Links to other websites

If you follow an external link, the destination website’s privacy practices apply. Review that website’s policy before providing personal information.

Children’s privacy

This informational website does not provide a way for children or other visitors to submit personal information.

Changes to this policy

If this website’s data practices change, this page will be updated with a new revision date.
